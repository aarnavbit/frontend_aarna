Glitch in the Gully

Glitch in the Gully

Glitch in the Gali reimagines the familiar chaos of Indian streets through a surreal digital lens. It captures the moment where the raw textures of a local gali collide with an unexpected system error, where tangled electric wires, roadside carts, and everyday street elements distort into neon gradients, pixelated forms, and floating fragments.

About Department

About Department

The Department of Design at IIT Hyderabad fosters a culture of curiosity, critical thinking, and creative exploration. With an interdisciplinary approach, the department encourages students to engage with design as a tool for innovation, problem-solving, and meaningful impact.
